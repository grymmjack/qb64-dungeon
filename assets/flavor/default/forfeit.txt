The dungeon claims another soul, and the dark closes over your name.
No song will be sung for you, no cairn raised. Only the cold, and the silence.
Your torch gutters out. The depths swallow the light, and then they swallow you.
So ends another delve -- one more skeleton among the countless, forgotten ones.
The gods look away. Your story is over, and no one remains to tell it.
You wanted gold and glory. The dungeon gave you only a grave.
Somewhere far above, the wind forgets your name. Down here, nothing remembers it at all.
Your knees buckle. The stone is cold. This is where the story quietly stops.
Braver souls have fallen here, and braver still will follow. You are simply another.
The last of your strength drains into the dust. The dark does not even notice.
