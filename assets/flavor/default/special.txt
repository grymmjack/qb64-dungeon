# room 4-5 richer, themed variations; one is picked at random. Edit freely.

MAIN GALLERY|The Main Gallery stretches wide and pillared, its vaulted dark swallowing your torchlight whole. Every path in this dungeon seems to bleed back to here.
MAIN GALLERY|Faded banners rot from the rafters of the Main Gallery, the crests of houses long extinct. Your footsteps ring out and are answered from every side.
MAIN GALLERY|The Main Gallery is the dungeon's cold heart -- a crossroads of shadow where a hundred corridors meet and none of them feel safe.
MAIN GALLERY|Broken statues line the Main Gallery, their faces chiseled away. Whoever ruled here wished to be remembered, and someone saw to it that they would not be.

ARMORY|Racks that once bristled with spears and blades stand empty and rust-eaten, though a few notched weapons still lie where they fell. Whatever army mustered here marched out long ago -- and never marched back.
ARMORY|The Armory walls are lined with dented shields bearing a sigil no living herald could name. Dark stains spread beneath each rack, as if the weapons themselves had bled.
ARMORY|Broken sword-hilts and shattered helms litter the Armory floor. A whetstone sits by a cold forge, still slick with oil, as though its keeper stepped away only moments ago.
ARMORY|Chainmail hangs from the Armory walls in curtains of rust, links fused by centuries of damp. Something, you note, has been sharpening its claws against the grindstone.

THE CRYPT|Row upon row of stone sarcophagi fill the Crypt, their lids carved with the faces of the long dead. One lid, you notice, has been pushed aside -- from within.
THE CRYPT|The Crypt air is dry and reverent, thick with the dust of ages and the sweet rot of old flowers. Names worn smooth by time cover every wall.
THE CRYPT|Candles that should have guttered out centuries ago still burn cold and blue throughout the Crypt. The dead here, it seems, are not so restful as they ought to be.
THE CRYPT|Bone-dust coats the Crypt floor in a fine grey powder -- and your footprints are not the only fresh ones. Something walks these tombs, and it does not sleep.

WIZ'S LAB|Shattered alembics and spilled reagents cover the Wizard's Lab, their fumes still curling in impossible colours. A half-finished sigil smoulders on the central table.
WIZ'S LAB|The Wizard's Lab hums with a residual, prickling power. Jars of things that should not float bob gently against their stoppers.
WIZ'S LAB|Star-charts and blasphemous diagrams paper every inch of the Lab's walls. In the centre, a scorched circle marks where some experiment went catastrophically wrong.
WIZ'S LAB|The air in the Wizard's Lab tastes of ozone and burnt hair. Something in a bell jar turns, slowly, to follow you as you cross the room.

WIZ'S TREASURE|The Wizard's hoard glitters with more than gold: rings that whisper, tomes bound in strange hide, and a crystal that shows you your own death. Best not to linger on that one.
WIZ'S TREASURE|Enchanted trinkets fill the Wizard's Treasure vault, each humming its own faint, discordant note. Together they make a music that sets your teeth on edge.
WIZ'S TREASURE|The Wizard's Treasure is guarded by wards you can feel but not see -- a static crawl across your skin with every step deeper in.
WIZ'S TREASURE|Gold here is the least of it. The Wizard's Treasure holds artefacts that bend the eye and coins that were never struck by any mortal mint.

KITCHEN|The great hearth of the Kitchen is cold now, but the spit above it still turns -- slow and creaking, though nothing tends it. On it roasts something you refuse to look at closely.
KITCHEN|Rusted cleavers and cauldrons crusted with unspeakable residue fill the Kitchen. Whatever was cooked here, it was not for gentle appetites.
KITCHEN|The Kitchen reeks of rancid grease and old smoke. Long tables run with dark stains, and the meat hooks overhead swing gently in a draft you cannot feel.
KITCHEN|A pot bubbles over the dead Kitchen fire, though no flame burns beneath it. The stew inside smells almost -- almost -- like something you would want to eat.

GUARD ROOM|The Guard Room is strewn with overturned stools and a card game abandoned mid-hand. The garrison left in a hurry -- or was taken.
GUARD ROOM|Rusted racks of pikes line the Guard Room, and a cold brazier still holds the ash of the last watch. Someone scratched a tally of days into the wall, then stopped.
GUARD ROOM|The Guard Room stinks of stale sweat and fear. A single boot lies in the corner, and the door is barred -- from the inside.
GUARD ROOM|Dice and copper coins lie scattered across the Guard Room floor, mid-throw. Whatever came for the watch, it gave them no time to finish the game.

STORE ROOM|The Store Room is packed with rotted crates and barrels swollen with age, their contents long since turned to mould and worse. Something scuttles behind the sacks.
STORE ROOM|Shelves sag under the weight of forgotten supplies in the Store Room, all of it spoiled, all of it crawling -- provisions for an army that never returned.
STORE ROOM|The Store Room air is thick with spores and the sweet reek of rot. Grain sacks have burst and grown pale, questing tendrils toward the light.
STORE ROOM|Broken barrels and split crates fill the Store Room, and in the dark between them, small eyes glint -- and multiply.

TORTURE CHAMBER|The Torture Chamber is a gallery of rusted horrors: racks and irons and things without names, all dark with old use. The screams have long faded, but the walls remember.
TORTURE CHAMBER|Chains hang from the Torture Chamber ceiling, ending in hooks that turn slowly of their own accord. The stone drain in the floor has never quite run clean.
TORTURE CHAMBER|Instruments of agony line the Torture Chamber walls in neat, obscene rows. A single chair sits in the centre, its straps worn smooth by desperate struggling.
TORTURE CHAMBER|The Torture Chamber air is heavy with dried blood and despair. Something scratches a slow, patient rhythm from inside the iron maiden.

QUEEN'S ANNEX|The Queen's Annex keeps a faded, poisonous elegance: rotted silk drapes, a cracked vanity, a music box that plays a single warped note when you pass.
QUEEN'S ANNEX|Perfume and decay war in the air of the Queen's Annex. A portrait on the wall has had its eyes carefully scratched out.
QUEEN'S ANNEX|The Queen's Annex is a boudoir gone to grave-mould, its finery grey with dust. The great bed is neatly made -- and something beneath it is very still.
QUEEN'S ANNEX|Withered rose petals carpet the Queen's Annex floor, and a hand mirror lies cracked upon the chaise. It shows no reflection at all.

QUEEN'S TREASURE|The Queen's Treasure spills across the floor in a river of jewels and pearls, each piece cold as a tomb. Her crown sits atop the pile, still faintly warm.
QUEEN'S TREASURE|Gowns sewn with gemstones moulder in the Queen's Treasure vault beside caskets of rings. Every gem seems to watch you with a small, avaricious gleam.
QUEEN'S TREASURE|The Queen's Treasure glitters with a beauty that feels like a trap. Necklaces of black pearls coil in their cases like sleeping serpents.
QUEEN'S TREASURE|Jewellery of impossible craft fills the Queen's Treasure -- and beneath the gold, the bones of everyone who came before you for it.

KING'S LIBRARY|The King's Library rises in tiers of rotting tomes and crumbling scrolls, their knowledge bleeding away into damp. One book lies open on a lectern, its ink still wet.
KING'S LIBRARY|Ten thousand books line the King's Library, and every one of them is screaming -- if you hold very still and listen. Best not to hold still.
KING'S LIBRARY|Dust and silverfish rule the King's Library now, but the wisdom of a fallen age still slumbers in its shelves. Some of it was never meant to be read.
KING'S LIBRARY|The King's Library air is thick with old vellum and older secrets. A ladder rolls slowly along the stacks, though no hand pushes it.

KING'S TREASURE|The King's Treasure is a dragon's dream: mountains of coin, chalices, crowns and sceptres heaped to the ceiling. All of it his, and all of it cursed.
KING'S TREASURE|Gold beyond counting fills the King's Treasure, so much it has slumped into glittering dunes. Greed prickles up your spine like a fever.
KING'S TREASURE|The King's Treasure could ransom a kingdom twice over. The King himself, they say, never left it -- and in a sense, he never did.
KING'S TREASURE|Coins cascade underfoot in the King's Treasure, and somewhere beneath the hoard, something ancient and possessive begins -- slowly -- to stir.
