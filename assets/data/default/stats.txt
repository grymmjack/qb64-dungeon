# ============================================================================
#  STATS -- what each ability score actually DOES, shown in the character
#  creator's side panel so the choice is informed.
#
#  FORMAT:  STAT | live | text
#    STAT   STR INT WIS DEX CON CHA
#    live   1 = the game does this TODAY.  0 = designed, not built yet.
#    text   one short line, <= 44 chars so it fits the panel
#
#  The `live` flag exists so this panel can never lie. A character creator that
#  promises "barter modifier" when nothing in the game barters is worse than
#  saying nothing -- the player spends points on a promise. Planned lines still
#  show, dimmed and marked, because knowing where a stat is HEADED is genuinely
#  useful when choosing what to raise. Flip the flag to 1 as each lands.
# ============================================================================

STR | 1 | +damage on every successful strike
STR | 1 | +to hit (except a Wizard, who swings by INT)
STR | 1 | force reinforced doors open
STR | 1 | physical saving throws
STR | 0 | wield heavier armour and melee weapons

DEX | 1 | bonus to armour class
DEX | 1 | movement + reflex saving throws
DEX | 0 | flourishes: spend timing for extra damage
DEX | 0 | accuracy with missile weapons

CON | 1 | extra HP on every level from here on
CON | 1 | elemental + endurance saving throws
CON | 0 | resistance to poison, acid, shock
CON | 0 | endure a maximum-damage blow for half

INT | 1 | a Wizard's to hit and damage
INT | 1 | mental saving throws
INT | 0 | how many spells you can carry
INT | 0 | speed of spell recall
INT | 0 | magic flourishes for greater effects

WIS | 1 | awareness saving throws
WIS | 0 | how far you can see and detect
WIS | 0 | bonus to finding hidden things
WIS | 0 | resistance to spell effects

CHA | 1 | luck points: re-roll a bad die
CHA | 0 | barter: what you pay and what you get
CHA | 0 | fate skew -- the dungeon's mood toward you
CHA | 0 | reaction saving throws
