# chamber-events.txt -- what happens when you step into a named CHAMBER.
#
# Per the board game a chamber holds THREE monsters and NO treasure, and you fight one
# per entry until three graves stand. This table adds VARIETY to those entries without
# breaking that rule: a non-gauntlet event happens INSTEAD of a fight on that entry, and
# the chamber still needs its three kills to clear.
#
# Anti-farming: at most ONE non-gauntlet event fires per chamber per run (CHM_EVDONE).
# Without that you could walk in and out of a hall collecting boons forever.
#
# Selection is a weighted, depth-gated draw (engine/TABLE.bas WeightPickLvl%): an entry
# only competes when the chamber's dungeon level falls in [minlvl, maxlvl]. maxlvl 0 means
# "and deeper", minlvl 0 means "from level 1" -- so a table can say "level 3+" without
# knowing how many levels exist.
#
# MECHANICS live in code, keyed by `kind` (same idiom as traps.txt / curios.txt) --
# everything else here is data. Known kinds:
#   gauntlet  one of the level's monsters rises (the classic behaviour; make this the
#             heaviest weight or chambers stop being dangerous)
#   shrine    a curio is drawn here instead (risk/reward choice -- see curios.txt)
#   hazard    a trap springs (see traps.txt)
#   boon      a cache: healing potion, or gold scaled to the chamber's depth
#   lord      a LORD of this hall -- a boss-flagged monster; it DOES count as a grave
#
# Weights are relative, not percentages. Re-read at launch (F5).
#
# kind     | name                  | weight | minlvl | maxlvl | text
gauntlet   | The guardians stir    | 62     | 0      | 0      | Something heavy shifts in the dark.
shrine     | A forgotten shrine    | 12     | 2      | 0      | A cracked altar leans against the far wall.
hazard     | The vault groans      | 10     | 3      | 0      | Dust sifts from the stones overhead.
boon       | A traveller's cache   | 10     | 1      | 0      | A niche in the wall hides someone's last supplies.
lord       | The lord of this hall | 6      | 5      | 0      | A presence claims this place, and it has noticed you.
