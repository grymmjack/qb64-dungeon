# ============================================================================
#  RIDDLES -- for the MAGIC MOUTH encounter (plans/PLANS.todo: "Magic mouths --
#  save WIS -- mini-game riddles").
#
#  FORMAT:  answer[/alt/alt] | riddle text
#    answer   the word(s) that count as correct. Slash-separated alternates all
#             count -- RiddleMatch% normalises case, punctuation, spaces and a
#             leading article, so "a shadow", "Shadow" and "shadows" all pass.
#    riddle   one line. Keep it under ~200 chars so it wraps inside the panel.
#
#  `#` comments and blank lines are ignored, like every other table here.
#
#  NOTE ON SOURCING: PLANS.todo points at github.com/crawsome/riddles
#  (riddles.csv). This box has no network access, so this is a hand-written
#  STARTER set in the dungeon's own voice. The loader is CSV-shaped enough that
#  importing that file later is a column swap, not a rewrite.
# ============================================================================

shadow          | I follow you all day long, but when the torch dies I am gone. What am I?
echo            | I speak without a mouth and answer only when spoken to. What am I?
darkness        | The more of me there is, the less you see. What am I?
candle/torch    | I am tall when young and short when old, and my one eye weeps as I die. What am I?
key             | I have teeth but cannot bite, and one turn of me opens the way. What am I?
coffin          | Six men carried me in, and only five walked back out. What am I?
silence         | Say my name and you have already broken me. What am I?
footsteps       | The more you take of them, the more you leave behind. What are they?
river           | I have a mouth but never eat, a bed but never sleep, and I run without legs. What am I?
map             | I have cities but no houses, mountains but no stone, and water but no fish. What am I?
bones           | The dungeon keeps me long after it has forgotten your name. What am I?
fire            | Feed me and I live; give me drink and I die. What am I?
egg             | A stone chest without hinge or key, and a golden treasure hides in me. What am I?
hole            | The more you take away from me, the bigger I become. What am I?
your name       | It belongs to you, yet everyone else uses it more than you do. What is it?
tomorrow        | I am always coming but I never arrive. What am I?
teeth           | Thirty white horses upon a red hill; they champ and stamp and then stand still. What are they?
wind            | You cannot see me, cannot hold me, but you feel me at every door. What am I?
door            | I am the one thing in this place that is both the way in and the way out. What am I?
gold            | Men dig through stone and die in the dark for me, and I have never once been worth it. What am I?
