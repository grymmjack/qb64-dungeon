DUNGEON!  --  MONSTER CARDS (kill numbers, transcribed from "Monster Cards.pdf")

Each card lists the 2d6 number you must MEET OR BEAT to slay the monster, per attacker.
"--" = cannot be slain by that attacker (needs a Magic Sword, or the right spell/melee).
Columns: Lightning Bolt | Fire Ball | Wizard | Superhero | Hero | Elf
(The game only uses the four melee columns -- Hero/Elf/Superhero/Wizard; a "--" there = 13.)
